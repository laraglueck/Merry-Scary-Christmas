# Spec: Bedroom (calendar, toy chest and photo) — V2

Owner: Bedroom developer

Status: Proposed — based on *Merry Scary Christmas – Story and Rooms, Team V2*, 17 November 2026

## Why

The bedroom is one of the three main puzzle rooms in Merry Scary Christmas.

The whole team enters and leaves the bedroom together. The bedroom provides the clue for the living room clock, contains a toy chest whose solution is revealed in the bathroom, and rewards the team with present 3 and the gold attic digit.

The bedroom also contains an old photograph of Scary Jingles and Santa. Collecting this photograph is required to unlock the optional Merry Christmas ending.

The implementation must use the existing shared game state, host-authoritative actions, inventory, countdown, save/resume and navigation systems.

## What

### 1. Scene and navigation

Create:

- `godot/scenes/bedroom.tscn`
- `godot/scripts/bedroom.gd`

The room contains:

- An iron bed with a blanket reaching the floor.
- A wooden toy chest with three symbol wheels.
- A wooden advent calendar with 12 numbered doors.
- Torn wallpaper.
- A snowy window.
- A door back to the hall.

Interactive areas:

- `calendar`
- `chest`
- `gift`
- `door_hall`
- `photo`, accessible through the calendar after the chest opens

The bedroom becomes accessible when the gold fuse is solved.

The whole team shares one current location. Entering or leaving the bedroom changes the shared location for all players.

The bedroom must use the existing room-navigation system instead of implementing a separate movement system.

All players see the same room and the same shared puzzle state.

### 2. Advent calendar: living room clue

The calendar contains 12 numbered doors.

Two doors reveal the solution for the stopped clock in the living room:

- One door contains the short clock hand.
- Another contains the long clock hand.

The door numbers match the living room's `hour` and `minute` positions.

The bedroom reads these positions from:

`game["living_room"]`

The positions are generated by the living room rules when a new game starts.

Requirements:

- Both positions must be between 1 and 12.
- The two positions must differ.
- The calendar must not generate a separate clock solution.
- The two clue doors are visible as open from the start.
- Clicking the calendar opens a close-up.
- The short and long hands must be visually distinguishable.
- The clue remains unchanged throughout the round.
- Viewing the calendar does not change the shared game state or apply a penalty.

Suggested pure function:

`calendar_doors(living_room)`

This returns the short-hand and long-hand door numbers from the existing living room state.

### 3. Toy chest: three-symbol lock

The toy chest has three independently rotating symbol wheels.

Each wheel cycles through:

- `star`
- `bell`
- `tree`
- `snowflake`

The correct combination is stored in:

`game["bedroom"]["wheels"]`

This field represents the secret solution, not the player's currently selected symbols.

The three symbols are randomly generated at game creation.

Repeated symbols are allowed. There are 64 possible combinations.

The bathroom mirror reads the bedroom solution from:

`game["bedroom"]["wheels"]`

After the bathroom tap puzzle is solved, the mirror displays the three symbols in reverse order, with each symbol visually mirrored.

Example:

Correct chest solution: `bell`, `star`, `tree`

Bathroom mirror order: `tree`, `star`, `bell`

The player cycles the three wheels locally and submits the selected combination by pressing the chest latch.

The submitted action is:

`set_wheels`

Example:

```gdscript
net.send_action("set_wheels", {
    "symbols": ["bell", "star", "tree"]
})
```

The host validates the submitted combination.

#### Correct attempt

- The chest opens.
- `game["bedroom"]["solved"]` becomes `true`.
- Present 3 appears.
- The gold attic digit becomes visible inside the chest lid.
- The remaining calendar doors become accessible.
- The chest stays open for the rest of the round.
- The updated state is synchronized to every player.

#### Wrong attempt

- The chest remains closed.
- The latch rattles.
- The team loses exactly 20 seconds.
- The player's current wheel selection remains visible.
- The correct solution remains unchanged.
- The player may try again.

#### Invalid attempt

Reject without penalty:

- Input is not an array.
- Input does not contain exactly three symbols.
- A symbol is unknown.
- The chest is already solved.
- The player is not authorized to interact with the bedroom.
- The round has already ended.

Invalid attempts must not change the puzzle or inventory.

### 4. Present 3 and gold attic digit

Opening the chest reveals present 3 and the digit engraved inside the lid.

The digit is generated once at game creation and is between 1 and 9.

It belongs to the gold dial of the attic lock.

Present 3 can be collected after the chest opens.

Action:

```gdscript
net.send_action("take_gift", {
    "room": "bedroom"
})
```

Requirements:

- The chest must be solved first.
- The present may be collected only once.
- The host updates `gift_taken`.
- The existing shared inventory displays present 3.
- The present disappears from the room after collection.
- All connected players receive the update.
- The digit remains visible after the present is collected.
- The present and digit remain correct after save/resume.

Do not implement a second inventory system inside the bedroom.

### 5. Photograph: required story item

The photograph is a mandatory feature.

The old photograph shows Scary Jingles and Santa together when they were happy.

It is hidden behind one of the ten calendar doors that are initially stuck.

The two clock-hand clue doors are always open.

The other ten doors remain locked until the toy chest has been solved.

After the chest opens:

- The ten remaining doors become accessible.
- One of them contains the photograph.
- The player can inspect and collect the photograph.
- The photograph is added to the team's shared inventory.
- The photograph can be collected only once.

Use:

`item_photo.png`

The photo door must not overlap with either clock-hand clue door.

The photograph's door position must be determined once at game creation and remain stable after save/resume.

Suggested shared state:

```gdscript
"bedroom": {
    "wheels": ["bell", "star", "tree"],
    "digit": 7,
    "solved": false,
    "gift_taken": false,
    "photo_door": 6,
    "photo_taken": false
}
```

The exact inventory representation must match the existing inventory implementation.

Suggested action:

```gdscript
net.send_action("take_photo", {
    "room": "bedroom"
})
```

Requirements:

- The photograph cannot be collected before the chest is solved.
- The photograph cannot be collected twice.
- The photograph is a shared team item.
- Collecting it updates the shared inventory.
- The photograph remains collected after leaving and re-entering the room.
- The photograph remains collected after save/resume.
- Collecting it does not apply a time penalty.

The attic ending logic uses the shared inventory to decide whether the Merry Christmas choice is available.

The bedroom does not implement the ending itself.

### 6. Shared bedroom state

Add the bedroom state through the existing game initialization process.

Required fields:

```gdscript
"bedroom": {
    "wheels": ["bell", "star", "tree"],
    "digit": 7,
    "solved": false,
    "gift_taken": false,
    "photo_door": 6,
    "photo_taken": false
}
```

The example values above are illustrative.

The actual values are generated using the shared random number generator.

Rules:

- The three-symbol solution is generated once per game.
- The gold digit is generated once per game.
- The photo door is selected from the ten non-clue doors.
- The state is owned and modified only by the host.
- All accepted changes are synchronized to the connected clients.
- The state is included in save/resume.
- Loading a saved game must not regenerate any of these values.

### 7. Bedroom rules

Create:

`godot/scripts/rules/bedroom_rules.gd`

Suggested functions:

**`create_room(rng)`**

Returns a new bedroom state containing the symbol solution, digit and initial completion flags.

The photograph's door position must be initialized consistently with the living room clock positions.

**`calendar_doors(living_room)`**

Returns the two clock clue positions using the living room's existing `hour` and `minute` fields.

**`try_wheels(room, symbols)`**

Validates a three-symbol chest attempt.

Use the same result conventions as the existing rules scripts, including `correct`, `penalty_seconds` and `error`.

**`take_gift(room)`**

Validates and records collection of present 3.

**`take_photo(room)`**

Validates and records collection of the photograph.

**`can_open_calendar_door(room, door_number, living_room)`**

Checks whether a numbered calendar door can be opened.

The two clock clue doors are accessible immediately. Other doors require the chest to be solved.

All rules must be independent of UI and networking.

### 8. Multiplayer integration

Use the existing host-authoritative architecture.

The client:

- Displays the shared bedroom state.
- Handles local wheel selection and close-up views.
- Sends actions through `net.send_action()`.
- Refreshes when `net.game_changed` is emitted.

The host:

- Validates player actions.
- Calls the bedroom rules.
- Updates the shared game state.
- Applies time penalties.
- Updates the shared inventory.
- Synchronizes accepted changes to all players.
- Saves the updated shared state through the existing save system.

Required actions:

- `set_wheels`
- `take_gift` with `room = "bedroom"`
- `take_photo`

The exact action names and result handling must be coordinated with the existing `GameRules.apply()` implementation.

Multiple players may click the same object. The host must ensure that a present or photo is awarded at most once.

The bedroom must use the existing shared location and reject actions when the team is not in the bedroom.

### 9. Countdown and penalties

The existing game countdown starts at 600 seconds.

A valid wrong chest attempt costs 20 seconds.

The bedroom must use the existing centralized penalty mechanism.

Do not create a separate timer.

Invalid actions do not cause penalties.

If the penalty reduces the remaining time to zero, the existing Midnight loss handling applies.

After the game has ended, bedroom interactions must no longer change the game state.

### 10. Save and resume

The bedroom must work with the existing save/resume feature.

The saved game must preserve:

- The three-symbol solution.
- The gold attic digit.
- Whether the chest is open.
- Whether present 3 has been collected.
- Which calendar door contains the photo.
- Whether the photo has been collected.
- The shared inventory.
- The shared location and remaining time, as managed centrally.

After resuming:

- The chest must not reset.
- Collected items must not reappear.
- The clock clue must still match the living room.
- The bathroom mirror must still match the bedroom solution.
- No new random values may be generated for an existing saved game.

The bedroom developer must not implement a separate save-file system.

### 12. Tests

Create:

`godot/tests/test_bedroom_rules.gd`

Follow the existing testing conventions.

#### Initialization

- A new room contains exactly three valid solution symbols.
- The same seed produces the same solution.
- The digit is between 1 and 9.
- The chest starts closed.
- The gift and photo start uncollected.
- The photo door is not a clock clue door.

#### Chest

- Correct symbols solve the chest.
- Wrong symbols do not solve the chest.
- A valid wrong attempt costs exactly 20 seconds.
- A wrong attempt does not change the secret solution.
- Invalid input causes no penalty.
- An unknown symbol is rejected.
- An array of incorrect length is rejected.
- Repeated valid symbols are allowed.
- A solved chest cannot be solved again.

#### Calendar

- There are 12 numbered doors.
- Exactly two clue doors are initially open.
- Both clue positions match the living room solution.
- The clue positions differ.
- The other ten doors cannot be opened before the chest is solved.
- The other doors become accessible after the chest opens.
- The photo door never overlaps with a clock clue door.

#### Present

- Present 3 cannot be taken before the chest opens.
- Present 3 can be taken after the chest opens.
- Present 3 cannot be collected twice.
- The gold digit remains available.

#### Photograph

- The photo cannot be collected before the chest opens.
- The photo can be collected after the chest opens.
- The photo can be collected exactly once.
- The photo appears in the shared inventory.
- The photo remains collected after save/resume.
- The attic ending system can recognize the photo through the shared inventory.

#### Multiplayer and persistence

- All players see the same solved state.
- All players see the same inventory.
- Two simultaneous collection actions cannot duplicate an item.
- Actions from outside the bedroom are rejected.
- Invalid actions do not change the shared game.
- A wrong attempt applies its penalty exactly once.
- Save/resume preserves the full bedroom state.
- The bedroom solution still matches the bathroom mirror after resume.
- The calendar still matches the living room after resume.

17. Find and collect the photo.
18. Verify that all players see both items in the shared inventory.
19. Leave and re-enter the bedroom.
20. Verify that the chest and item states are preserved.
21. Save and resume the game.
22. Verify that all bedroom progress remains unchanged.
23. Verify that the attic ending system recognizes the photo.
